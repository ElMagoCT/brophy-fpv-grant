Lowering the barrier to entry

Anyone can start flying today, for free

  • The sim lab. Five FPV simulators in the IC, open 7:00–4:00 on school days. Tap your name, pick a sim, fly. Crashes cost nothing.
  • No seat? Ground School. 43 lessons on the kiosk or any phone. Drills on the real transmitter; progress follows the pilot.
  • Zero cost to the student. Sim hours, lessons, quizzes and FAA TRUST are free. Club gear is shared and checked out.
  • Clear first steps. Name at the kiosk → 7.5 sim hours → Safe Flight and Battery Care quizzes → build and flash a Meteor → first real flight.
the kiosk · open to anyone
Structure that ensures learning

Badges unlock gear

  • A badge is one specific, testable skill. Earn every badge in a tier and the next drone is yours to fly. "Who may fly what" is written down, not remembered.
  • Demonstration, not attendance. Quiz badges: 10 questions, 80% passes, the kiosk awards them. Bench badges: checklist, photo of the work, instructor approves. Witnessed badges: a mentor watches you meet the written standard. FAA TRUST on file.
  • The record. One badge catalogue shared by kiosk and website. Every award signed by the approving instructor. Progress only moves forward.
Structure that ensures safety

A written safety standard

  • Brophy FPV safe-flight rules, published and quizzed. Passing them is the Safe Flight badge.
  • Nothing over 250 g until the top tier.
  • No real drone before Safe Flight and Battery Care. No event flying before FAA TRUST.
  • Event protocol: a spotter on every flight, a flight zone set up, bystanders briefed, failsafe tested, ducted airframes near people.
  • Batteries: balance-charge, storage voltage, puffed-pack check, smoke stopper on every first power-up, fire procedure drilled.
  • Airspace: B4UFLY check, LAANC when needed under Sky Harbor's Class B, incident report the same day.

Structure that ensures sustainability

Built to outlive its founders

  • Skills normally graduate with the seniors. Here they are badges, and the club is heavily underclassmen, gaining members every week.
  • Mentor: coach a new member through two badges, then sign off the badges you hold, with officer approval. The program produces its own teachers every year.
  • Everything is written down: the badge sheet, the safety rules, seven printed posters, a website that runs itself with live lab numbers.
  • Kiosk and lessons are already built and maintained by students.
  • Recruiting moments: the fall assembly pitch, a pep-rally race the whole school watches through the goggles, posters above the sims, a public website anyone can open.

Other yields for Brophy

What else the school gets

Best of Brophy

Short aerial clips through the year for games, dances and clubs, cut into two showcase films a year for admissions, assemblies and social media.

Already in the field

We have already flown and filmed at a couple of school events this year, with a spotter and the event protocol. The pipeline exists; this scales it.

Community-period races

The pep rally, "Written in the Stars": a live FPV race on the scoreboard. The same course runs as a core-programming and a lunch race, flown only by Racing-badge pilots.

Interest is already here

Stuco, the racing club, admissions and sports have asked for footage. Teams get clips they can use the same week.

A model other clubs can copy

Badge → gate → gear is not drone-specific. Robotics, shop, A/V: any club with expensive tools and a safety question can run the same system.
